# RCS Capital (rcs-capital)
RCS Capital Corporation was a holding company focused on the financial services industry that owned a diversified group of businesses including independent broker-dealers and investment banking. The firm filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in January 2016 and emerged as Aretec Group, Inc. No public APIs are available for the legacy RCS Capital entity.
